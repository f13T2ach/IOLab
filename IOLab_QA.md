# IOLab 开发历程 QA

Q: 为什么设计 IOLab？

A: IOLab 是团队高中时期带头教师的设想的具体实现。该设想此前由团队学长实现过其中一个模块--pH计，本团队以市面上已经存在的“朗威”系列教学产品为参考，设计出了具有更现代功能的实验产品 IOLab。

Q: 开发 IOLab 花了多久？

A: 从24年暑假开始开发，总耗时约八个月，这其中耗时最多的是程序设计部分。在参加于横琴举行的粤港澳比赛之前，还经过了4个月的微调，我们购置了校准液等材料，用于校准传感器。尽管精度不高，但是 IOLab 注重的是可拓展性，这也是 IOLab 的设计思想。

Q: 你（团队）所提到的 “可拓展性” 体现在什么方面？

A: 可拓展性体现在两个方面：程序架构可拓展性与用户创意可拓展性。前者主要为下位机程序的传感器接口实现，用户可以设计传感器的驱动程序，并把接口输入、数据输出接入自己的程序中。后者主要为上位机的一些巧思，比如“实验”板块的可拓展性，用户可以发布自己设计的实验，让大家都可以下载来实践。

Q: 你（团队）申请过专利吗？

A: 团队申请过，但是因为说明书的问题，被协助提交专利的机构多次驳回。学业紧张，遂没有继续申请。

# IOLab Development Journey QA

Q: Why was IOLab designed?

A: IOLab is the concrete realization of an idea proposed by our lead teacher during our high school years. A previous version of one module—the pH meter—had been implemented by senior students in our team. Our team referenced existing teaching products on the market like the "Langwei" series to design IOLab, an experimental product with more modern functionalities.

Q: How long did it take to develop IOLab?

A: Development started in the summer of 2024 and took approximately eight months in total. The most time-consuming part was programming. Before participating in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao competition held in Hengqin, we spent an additional four months on fine-tuning. We purchased materials like calibration solutions to calibrate the sensors. Although the precision isn't extremely high, IOLab focuses on extensibility, which is its core design philosophy.

Q: In what aspects is the "extensibility" you (the team) mentioned reflected?

A: The extensibility is reflected in two main aspects: Program Architecture Extensibility and User Creativity Extensibility.

The former primarily involves the implementation of sensor interfaces in the firmware (lower-computer program). Users can design drivers for sensors and integrate the input interfaces and data output into their own programs.

The latter mainly consists of clever features in the software (upper-computer program), such as the extensibility of the "Experiments" section. Users can publish their own designed experiments for others to download and practice.

Q: Did you (the team) apply for a patent?

A: The team did apply, but the application was rejected multiple times by the agency assisting with the submission due to issues with the specification documents. Due to academic pressure, we did not continue the application process.
